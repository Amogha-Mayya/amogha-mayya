    About Me

    I'm Amogha Mayya, an AI/ML Engineer pursuing my M.Tech in Computer Science and Information Security at NIT Karnataka. My work sits at the intersection of deep learning, generative AI, and applied research — building systems that don't just process data, but genuinely understand it.

    Previously, I worked as a Data Analyst at Riskonnect Inc., where I designed ETL pipelines, wrote complex SQL transformations, and delivered data-driven insights across multiple enterprise clients. That foundation in data engineering gives me a strong appreciation for building robust, production-ready ML systems.

    Today, I focus on building AI agent workflows and developing deep learning models in PyTorch. I enjoy turning ideas into practical AI systems, with an emphasis on scalable architectures, experimentation, and real-world deployment.
